About the Film

         

        Golden Wings chronicles the remarkable career of Robyn Stewart, an American Airlines flight attendant whose 50+ years in the skies witnessed the transformation of commercial aviation.      

         

        Through intimate interviews and archival footage, this documentary captures stories of courage, dedication, and the evolution of an industry that connected the world.
